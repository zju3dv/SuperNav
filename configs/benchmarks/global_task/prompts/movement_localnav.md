- hab_local_navigate(view="front", point=[0.5,0.8], goal_id=...) - the ONLY translation tool in this arm: a learned RGB policy walks to the pixel you mark on the latest front/right/back/left view, one bounded hop per call, no preview/confirm step. How to pick points, read statuses, and recover from failures is documented in the localnav-pointnav skill — follow it.
- Arm constraints: hab_forward, hab_backward, hab_visual_local_navigate, hab_visual_point_navigate, hab_visual_overlay_navigate, and hab_navigate_wam are not available here. All translation goes through hab_local_navigate; hab_turn only rotates in place; hab_panorama only re-captures the surround.
